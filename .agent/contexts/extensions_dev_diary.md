## 2026-04-07 — Epistemic Module: Conceptual Architecture (Ontology Agent Response)

> *The following is a response from the ontology development agent, recorded here as the founding design rationale for the epistemic module.*

The epistemic module addresses one of the most persistent issues in energy system modeling: the "black box" of assumptions. Models are often mathematically rigorous but epistemologically opaque. By formalizing provenance, authorship, and rationale, a raw dataset becomes a fully traceable knowledge artifact.

Grounded in the OBO Foundry ecosystem (BFO, OEO, IAO), the module brings in **PROV-O**, **ECO (Evidence & Conclusion Ontology)**, and **IAO (Information Artifact Ontology)**. The design is structured around four pillars.

---

### Pillar 1 — Agency and Attribution (Who is responsible?)

Captures *who* or *what* created the model, scenarios, or specific parameters. In BFO terms: independent continuants (persons, organizations, software agents).

- **External ontology:** PROV-O (+ optionally FOAF/Dublin Core)
- **Key classes:**
  - `prov:Agent` — overarching class; subclasses: `prov:Person`, `prov:Organization`, `prov:SoftwareAgent`
- **Key relations:**
  - `prov:wasAttributedTo` — links a `CalliopeScenario` or parameter (Information Content Entity) to the responsible agent

---

### Pillar 2 — Lineage and Derivation (Where did the data come from?)

Tracks the lifecycle of information artifacts: how data was processed, scaled, or derived from a parent dataset.

- **External ontology:** PROV-O
- **Key classes:**
  - `prov:Activity` — an occurrent representing an action on data (e.g., "Data Normalization", "Spatial Aggregation", "Literature Review")
- **Key relations:**
  - `prov:wasDerivedFrom` — links a parameter (e.g., final solar capacity factor profile) to a parent `oeo:DataSet` (e.g., raw Renewables.ninja data)
  - `prov:wasGeneratedBy` — links the parameter to the `prov:Activity` that created it
  - `prov:used` — links the activity to its original inputs

---

### Pillar 3 — Justification and Assumptions (Why this value?)

Holds the modeler's qualitative rationale. Why 25 years for wind turbine lifetime instead of 30? These textual justifications need formal representation.

- **External ontology:** IAO
- **Key classes:**
  - `ontocal:ModelingAssumption` — new class, subclass of `iao:InformationContentEntity`, holds plain-text justification or mathematical logic
- **Key relations:**
  - `iao:is_about` — the assumption *is about* the specific parameter
  - `dcterms:references` / `iao:cites` — links the assumption to a publication, DOI, or report

---

### Pillar 4 — Epistemic Status / Evidence (How certain are we?)

Not all data is created equal: a capacity limit may be a hard physical constraint, a policy target, or a rough expert guess. Capturing this "flavor of truth" is crucial for interpreting model rigidity.

- **External ontology:** ECO (Evidence & Conclusion Ontology)
- **Key classes (examples):**
  - `eco:0000033` — Traceable Author Statement ("I read this in an IPCC report")
  - `eco:0000008` — Empirical Evidence ("from a smart meter dataset")
  - `eco:0000352` — Evidence used in manual assertion ("heuristic guess based on expertise")

---

### Conceptual Walkthrough

A scientist creates a `CalliopeModelParameter` for the capital cost of a solar panel (`CostFlowCap`). The epistemic module would describe it as:

1. The `CostFlowCap` parameter **is about** (`iao:is_about`) the `CalliopeSupplyTechnology` (Solar PV)
2. It **was attributed to** (`prov:wasAttributedTo`) a `prov:Person` (Dr. Smith)
3. It **was derived from** (`prov:wasDerivedFrom`) an external `oeo:DataSet` (NREL ATB 2023)
4. It **was generated by** (`prov:wasGeneratedBy`) a `prov:Activity` (Currency Conversion & Inflation Adjustment)
5. An `ontocal:ModelingAssumption` **is about** (`iao:is_about`) this parameter, with text: *"Adjusted NREL 2023 costs to 2025 EUR assuming 2% inflation."*
6. The parameter has evidence type `eco:0000033` (Traceable Author Statement), linking out to the NREL DOI
